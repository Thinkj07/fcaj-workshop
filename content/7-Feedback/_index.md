# Program Experience Sharing & Feedback Report

Following an incredibly rewarding internship journey with the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program, I would like to express my sincere appreciation to the Organizing Committee, Mentors, and the entire FCAJ team, while offering constructive feedback based on my practical experience.

---

### Detailed Program Evaluation

**1. Working Environment & Academic Atmosphere**  
The working environment at the AWS office (Bitexco Financial Tower, 26th Floor) is highly professional, modern, and exceptionally open. A strong spirit of teamwork and academic enthusiasm permeates the workplace. Both mentors and peers were consistently accessible to answer technical questions and provide architectural guidance whenever challenges arose.

**2. Mentorship & Admin Support**  
Mentors adopted an "empowerment and guidance" approach—encouraging interns to analyze root problems, experiment with architectural patterns, and learn from mistakes rather than providing ready-made answers. The Admin team managed logistics, documentation, AWS account provisioning, and event organization seamlessly.

**3. Alignment with Academic Major**  
The internship curriculum aligned exceptionally well with foundational computer science courses such as Computer Networking, Operating Systems, and Database Management. The program elevated theoretical concepts to enterprise-grade scales through hands-on implementation on AWS services like Multi-AZ VPCs, RDS Primary/Standby DBs, Serverless Lambda, CloudFront CDN, and App Runner.

**4. Comprehensive Skill Development**  
FCAJ offered a balanced growth path for both hard and soft skills: from cloud architecture design, proposal writing, and workshop lab execution to technical writing (authoring 3 published blog posts) and presenting under pressure during competitive events (Cloud Architect Game Show, FCAJ × AABW).

**5. Respectful Culture & Community Spirit**  
The work culture at FCAJ is built on mutual respect. Regardless of intern status, design ideas and technical solutions were actively listened to, constructively critiqued, and valued by senior mentors.

**6. Internship Support & Opportunities**  
The program provided generous hands-on access to AWS resources, flexible scheduling to accommodate academic commitments, and opportunities to connect directly with leading industry experts and community builders.

---

### Key Reflection Questions

- **What did you find most satisfying during your internship?**  
  My proudest achievement was independently crafting a comprehensive **E-Commerce Perfume Store Backend** architectural proposal aligned with AWS Best Practices, as well as experiencing cutting-edge Agentic AI solutions live at the **FCAJ × AABW** event.

- **What should the company improve for future intern cohorts?**  
  I recommend incorporating more **Group Architectural Workshops** (in-person collaborative design sessions) or **Troubleshooting Challenges** (simulated incident response labs) to foster deeper team interaction and sharpen real-time diagnostic skills.

- **Would you recommend this program to your peers?**  
  **100% Yes.** FCAJ is one of the premier cloud and AI internship programs, offering a clear practical roadmap, a professional work environment, and exceptional mentorship from AWS engineers.

---

### Future Suggestions & Aspirations

- **Program Development Proposal:** Establish a centralized Knowledge Base featuring baseline architectural blueprints and FAQ repositories compiled from past cohorts to streamline onboarding for future interns.
- **Future Engagement:** I am eager to remain connected with the **FCAJ** and **AWS Study Group** communities as an active Alumnus or Technical Contributor for upcoming events and workshops.
